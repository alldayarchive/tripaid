Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

1. Data Minimization

TripAid is a "Zero-Server" utility. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal identification data to our servers. All information you provide (like hotel names or addresses) is stored exclusively on your device's local storage.

2. Real-time Location (GPS)

Our app requests GPS access to provide real-time navigation links via Naver/Google Maps. This data is processed locally to generate search queries and is never recorded or tracked by TripAid.

3. Third-Party Services

We provide links to external services (Naver Maps, NordVPN, Saily, etc.). These services have their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for the data practices of these external platforms.
